Quinceañera coordination questions

What does a quinceañera planner or coordinator do?

Ana learns your plan in the months before the quince, builds a detailed timeline, confirms every vendor, and then runs the day itself: the Misa lineup, vendor setup, the court’s entrances and dances, the traditions and the wrap-up. You make the decisions; she carries them out.

How much does a quinceañera coordinator cost?

Ana quotes every quinceañera individually, because the cost depends on your date, your venue, how many hours the day needs (up to 6 on site) and how much planning support you want beforehand. Send your date and venue and she’ll get back to you with a quote.

When should we book a quinceañera coordinator?

As soon as you have your date and venue. Ana works with your family in the months before the quince, so booking early gives her time to meet your vendors and build the timeline with you.

Do you help with the court of damas and chambelanes?

Yes. Ana builds the court’s cues into the timeline and manages them on the day: lineup for the Misa and the entrance, the vals, the surprise dance and anything else they’re part of.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. Ana coordinates in English and Spanish, with your family, your padrinos and your vendors.

Where do you coordinate quinceañeras?

At venues and churches throughout Dallas–Fort Worth.
