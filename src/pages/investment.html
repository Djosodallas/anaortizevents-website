Common questions

How much does a day-of wedding coordinator cost?

Ana quotes every wedding and quinceañera individually, because the cost depends on your date, your venue, how many hours the day needs (up to 6 on site) and how much planning support you want in the months before. Send your date and venue and she’ll get back to you with a quote.

When should I book a day-of coordinator?

As soon as you have your date and venue. Ana starts working with you in the months before your wedding or quince, so booking early gives her time to meet your vendors and build the timeline with you.

What’s the difference between a wedding planner and a day-of coordinator?

You choose and book your own vendors. Ana helps with recommendations and design, then takes over communication with your vendors, builds the timeline and runs the day so nothing falls back on your family.

Do you coordinate weddings and quinceañeras?

Yes. Weddings and quinceañeras are Ana’s specialty, and the package is the same for both: planning in the months before and up to 6 hours on the day. For other celebrations, reach out with your date and details.
