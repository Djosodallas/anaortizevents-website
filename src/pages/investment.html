Common questions

When should I book a day-of coordinator?

As soon as you have your date and venue. Ana starts working with you in the months before the quince, so booking early gives her time to meet your vendors and build the timeline with you.

What’s the difference between a planner and a day-of coordinator?

You choose and book your own vendors. Ana helps with recommendations and design, then takes over communication with your vendors, builds the timeline and runs the day so nothing falls back on your family.

Do you only work quinceañeras?

Quinceañeras are Ana’s specialty. For other celebrations, reach out with your date and details.
