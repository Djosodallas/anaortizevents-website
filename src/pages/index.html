Where Ana makes a difference

Every wedding and quince has its own order of events. Here’s how a typical day runs with Ana on site.

  1. The ceremony

    Whether it’s a wedding ceremony or a Misa de Acción de Gracias, Ana confirms the timing, lines up the wedding party or court and makes sure the photographer and padrinos are where they need to be.

  2. Arrival and setup at the venue

    She meets every vendor as they arrive, checks décor against your plan and places your personal pieces: guest book, cake knife, cushions, the last doll, family photos.

  3. Grand entrance

    The wedding party or court is cued, the DJ has the right song, and the couple or the quinceañera walks in on time.

  4. First dances and traditions

    The first dance, the father-daughter dance, the vals, the shoe change: every tradition happens in order, with everyone who has a role ready before their moment.

  5. Toasts, cake and the party

    Ana keeps the program moving so the celebration never stalls between moments.

  6. Last dance and wrap-up

    She gathers your gifts and personal décor and confirms vendor breakdown, so your family can leave with nothing left to do.
