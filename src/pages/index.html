Where Ana makes a difference

Every quince has its own order of events. Here’s how a typical day runs with Ana on site.

  1. Misa de Acción de Gracias

    Ana confirms the church timing, lines up the court and makes sure the photographer and padrinos are where they need to be.

  2. Arrival and setup at the venue

    She meets every vendor as they arrive, checks décor against your plan and places your personal pieces: the doll, the shoes, the cushions, the photos.

  3. Grand entrance

    The court is cued, the DJ has the right song, and the quinceañera walks in on time.

  4. Traditions and the vals

    Cambio de zapatos, última muñeca, the father-daughter dance and the waltz happen in order, with everyone who has a role ready before their moment.

  5. Surprise dance, toast and cake

    Ana keeps the program moving so the party never stalls between moments.

  6. Last dance and wrap-up

    She gathers your gifts and personal décor and confirms vendor breakdown, so your family can leave with nothing left to do.
