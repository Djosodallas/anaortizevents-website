What Ana believes about the big day

Your family belongs in the photos

The people who planned the day should be dancing, not checking on the caterer. Ana takes the questions so Mom, Dad, the couple and the padrinos don’t have to.

A plan only works if someone runs it

Months of decisions come down to a few hours. Ana learns your plan before the day, then makes sure every vendor and every person with a role follows it.

Traditions deserve to be done right

The lazo and arras, the Misa, the court, the shoe change and the last doll each have their moment. Ana makes sure everyone involved is ready before it starts.

How working with Ana goes

From your first message to the last dance.

  1. Tell her your date and venue

    Send a message through the contact form, or call or text 469-970-4473. Ana replies with her availability and a quote for your celebration.

  2. Plan together in the months before

    Unlimited calls, texts and email, virtual planning meetings and a walkthrough of your venue with décor and design guidance.

  3. She talks to your vendors

    Ana confirms details with every vendor and, if you still need one, recommends people she trusts.

  4. Your timeline, written down

    A custom, detailed timeline for the day, reviewed together at a final details meeting before the event.

  5. The day itself

    Up to 6 hours of on-site coordination: vendor setup, your personal pieces, the wedding party or court, every tradition and the wrap-up.
